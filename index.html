Introduction

I’m James Watson, a translator and reviewer with 10 years’ experience working with technical documentation across mechanical engineering, automotive and IT.

Alongside language work, I build small tools and workflows for quality assurance, data handling, automation and technical problem-solving. Quietly Technical is where I document some of that work and the thinking behind it.

What I do

Language & QA

Translation, editing, localisation and quality assurance, with a particular interest in consistency, usability and the details that make finished work trustworthy.

Systems & automation

Small tools, repeatable workflows and practical automation that remove friction, reduce avoidable errors and make complicated work easier to manage.

Technical communication

Turning technical material into something another person can understand and use, whether that means documentation, explanation, troubleshooting or process design.
